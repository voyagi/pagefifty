Why a partner can close it

Directive 2014/24/EU, Article 63(1): a firm may rely on the capacities of other entities for technical and professional ability, and for professional experience "only ... where the latter will perform the works or services for which these capacities are required". The buyer may still reserve critical tasks for you (Article 63(2)). This notice names none, but it leaves the details of relying on another firm to the document pack, which is not read yet. Each partner files a commitment to provide the capacity and its own exclusion declaration with your application.
