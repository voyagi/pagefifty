The start page picture: library-wide-*.webp and library-tall-*.webp

A generated image made for Pagefifty with ChatGPT from our own prompt (October 2026). It shows an
imaginary library, not a real building, and it has no people, signs or text in it. The exact prompt
sits in the .json file beside each image.

How the files were made from the generated picture (1672 x 936 PNG):
- Upscaled to 3344 x 1872 with waifu2x-ncnn-vulkan release 20250915 (MIT licence), model
  upconv_7_photo, scale 2, noise level 0.
  Every part was compared at full size with the original: nothing was added or redrawn.
- library-wide-*: the whole picture, resized with Lanczos to 1280, 1920, 2560 and 3344 wide.
- library-tall-*: a portrait crop for phones, 1521 x 1872 from x 940 of the upscaled picture
  (the staircase and the green seats), resized to 800 and 1200 wide.
- Encoded with cwebp at quality 80, sharp YUV, no metadata.
